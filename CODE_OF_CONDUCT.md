# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing about code.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; critique the change, not the person.
- Assume good faith and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of spam or self-promotion.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone’s private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub’s
[private vulnerability reporting][advisory] or by opening a confidential
request through the repository owner’s GitHub profile. Reports are reviewed
promptly and handled confidentially.

Consequences may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction,
or a permanent ban, depending on severity.
